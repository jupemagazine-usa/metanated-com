Tourism & visits

Tour the Museum

Visits, schools & groups.

The Post Content Museum is a working archive inside a working studio. Visitors walk the same path a record walks: intake at the gates, capture in the workshop, clearance at the broadcast desk, the building face outside, and the record itself in the vault.

Node Zero — 7367 Chef Menteur Hwy, New Orleans · Duration to be confirmed · Groups and schools welcome.

Visits are by confirmed reservation. Submitting a request does not reserve a time until our team confirms the arrangements. Visitors checked in on completed tours are counted in the recorded attendance; booking requests are not counted.
