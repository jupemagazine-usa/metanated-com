03

What has to be registered

File the work, its rights and its supporting record.

IndustryRecord / registrationFiling standard
Music & recordingPRO and publisher accounts; ISWC, ISRC, composition and recording copyright; signed splits.File contributor agreements and recording identifiers.
Film, TV & B-rollProduction entity; applicable incentive filings; talent and location releases; chain of title.Accession a signed, timecoded shot list.
Publishing & written workCopyright; ISBN and imprint where applicable; publisher of record; subsidiary rights.Attach rights summaries and display restrictions.
Podcast & broadcastShow entity; sponsor disclosures; music and clip licenses; guest consent; retained transcripts.Attach the clearance file to the episode.
Advertising & physical signageBusiness license; applicable permits; structural and electrical review; lease terms; proof of run.File artwork, permits and proof of run together.
Design, software & inventionApplicable trademark and patent filings; source escrow; contributor assignments.Record assignments with the catalog entry.
Business, tax & estateEntity formation; EIN; state business ID; applicable business and tax registrations; wills and trusts.Keep sensitive identifiers in the private record.

Requirements vary by jurisdiction. Your licensed professional reviews applicable filings; Metanated holds the record.

04

The controls

Review, coverage and reserves sit alongside the work.

Professional controls

Assign a professional of record. Separate filing, review and approval. Log credits, splits, clearances and dated corrections.

Coverage & risk

File policies, certificates and exposures with each work or booking. Record coverage terms, renewal dates and succession instructions.

Money set aside first

Record reserves, tax set-asides, coverage costs, filing fees and legacy allocations in the operating budget.

The School of the Registry

  1. Read Every Contract — Review terms, territory, recoupment, splits, reversion, audit rights and assignment.
  2. Register Before You Release — File identifiers, contributors and applicable registrations before release.
  3. Own the Master, Keep the Pub — Record master ownership, publishing shares and licensing terms.
  4. Build the Table, Then Eat — Assign legal, accounting, representation and stewardship roles.
  5. Distribute Wealth, Not Just Songs — Document collaborator credits, compensation and participation.
  6. Inherit Forward — File beneficiaries, successors and instructions for the catalog.
