The Apprenticeship Playbook

From the brief to the receipt

01 / Open — Walk the room

Studio or brand: Write the brief before posting: the intended result, deliverables, budget, dates, equipment, and rights required. Choose where to share it once the scope is ready.

Creative partner: Check the scope against your skills and capacity. Identify missing information before quoting.

Keep the record: A dated brief with a named decision-maker, budget range, deadline, and rights requirements.

This hub helps prepare the brief; it does not publish jobs or solicit bids.

02 / Morning — Clear the board

Studio or brand: Browse creator-listed work and review relevant examples. Discuss availability, rates, revisions, and ownership before choosing a partner.

Creative partner: Show relevant work and confirm the deliverables, schedule, fee, and payment terms in writing.

Keep the record: An agreed scope and written terms accepted by both parties.

A creator shelf shows work, not verified availability. Browsing is not a match or a confirmed engagement.

03 / Midday — The work itself

Studio or brand: Name the project owner and today's deliverable. Confirm room access, schedule, and review points before the session.

Creative partner: Run the agreed work, follow studio etiquette, and flag changes before adding time or cost.

Keep the record: The confirmed schedule, session notes, and approved scope changes.

A studio booking request is not a confirmed session until accepted.

04 / Afternoon — Credit and seal

Studio or brand: Review contributor credits, identifiers, rights, and clearance requirements before release.

Creative partner: Name files consistently, complete the metadata audit, seal the deliverable, and retain its manifest.

Keep the record: Credits, rights statement, identifiers, content hash, and an accession ticket.

A seal documents a file; it does not grant copyright or replace permissions.

05 / Close — Reset and log

Studio or brand: Review the delivery against the brief. Record acceptance or specific revisions and confirm where the final assets are kept.

Creative partner: Log hours and materials, document the handoff, verify backups, and reset the room for the next person.

Keep the record: Acceptance, the revision log, asset locations, and the project ticket.

Keep the signal and receipt together: what was made, who made it, and what was accepted.

06 / End of day — Count

Studio or brand: Check the invoice against agreed terms and accepted work. Pay through the agreed external method and retain the payment reference.

Creative partner: Invoice promptly with the agreed amount, currency, due date, and payment instructions. Follow up on unpaid invoices and reconcile money received.

Keep the record: Invoice, due date, expenses, payment reference, and a clear invoiced / unpaid / received status.

An invoice is not money received. This hub neither collects nor distributes payment; no checkout or payouts are enabled.
